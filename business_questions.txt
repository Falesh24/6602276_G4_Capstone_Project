USED-CAR MARKET ANALYSIS

Analysis Objective
Analyse 6,000+ used-car listings to understand pricing, availability, and key vehicle characteristics, and derive evidence-based insights.

Business Questions — Excel Analysis
1. MARKET LANDSCAPE
- How many cars are available per year?
- What is the distribution of cars by transmission type?
- Which owner type has the most listings, and what is its average selling price?

2. PRICING ANALYSIS
- What is the average selling price of used cars by brand?
- Which year has the highest average selling price?

3. VEHICLE ECONOMICS
- What is the average mileage (kmpl/km/kg) by fuel type?
- What is the relationship between engine size (binned) and price?
